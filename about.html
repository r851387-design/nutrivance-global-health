ABOUT NUTRIVANCE

Healthier lives through knowledge and action.

NutriVance Global Health is a health education and wellness initiative focused on nutrition, public health learning, research support and community wellbeing. Our goal is to make health knowledge practical, accessible and respectful of people's circumstances.

Our purpose

We aim to support individuals, community groups, schools, health organisations and other partners through evidence-informed learning and practical health promotion.

Our values

  • Evidence-informed: use credible information and communicate it clearly.
  • People-centred: respect lived experience, culture and local context.
  • Practical impact: focus on realistic actions and continuous improvement.
  • Inclusive: make health education useful for diverse audiences.
