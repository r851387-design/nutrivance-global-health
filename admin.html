Content management

Website dashboard

Manage articles and uploaded images.

Setup required: this dashboard becomes functional only after you create a Supabase project, run supabase-setup.sql, and enter your project's URL and publishable/anon key in admin-config.js. Never enter a service-role key here.

Connect the secure backend

Open admin-config.js and replace the two placeholder values with your Supabase Project URL and publishable (anon) key. These are designed for browser use when Row Level Security is correctly configured.

Admin account allowed by the database policy: nutrivance3@gmail.com

Administrator sign in

Use the account you authorised in Supabase Authentication.

Create an article

Use JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF. Large images may take longer to upload. The database storage policy restricts uploads to your authorised account.

Your articles

Drafts are private to you. Published articles are available to public readers when the public article page is connected to this database.

Image uploads

Images uploaded while editing an article are stored in the NutriVance media bucket. Use the image field above to upload and attach an image to a post.
